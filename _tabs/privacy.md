## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **MACH Playbook** ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Lenin Meza at [https://mach-playbook.github.io](https://mach-playbook.github.io/), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We prioritize user privacy and minimize data collection:
* **Analytics Data:** We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymous traffic metrics (page views, session duration, browser type, device type, geographic country). No personally identifiable information (PII) is stored or tracked.
* **Contact Submissions:** If you voluntarily reach out through our [Contact Page](/contact/) or email, we only use your name and email address to reply to your inquiry.

---

### 2. Cookies and Google AdSense
This site uses cookies for web analytics and advertising services through **Google AdSense**.

* Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
* Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
* **Opt-Out:** Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google's [Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies by visiting [aboutads.info choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).

---

### 3. Third-Party Services and Subprocessors
* **Google Analytics & Google AdSense:** Visitor metrics and ad serving under Google Privacy Policies.
* **Formspree:** Secure handling of contact form submissions.

### 4. Data Protection, GDPR & CCPA Compliance
Under GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act):
* You have the right to request information regarding any personal data we process.
* You have the right to request deletion of any communication data you submitted to us.
* We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to any third parties.
